1. Summary

  • Most Games have no accounts or sign-in. Your progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • We never ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location in our Games. We only receive your email address if you email us.
  • Depending on the Game, it may show ads (Google AdMob), use Google Firebase to understand how the Game is played and to fix crashes, and sell optional items through the app store's billing system.
  • Some Games have an online account and save data on our servers. These are described in section 6.
  • Analytics is collected only after you consent where consent is required. In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choices at any time in the Game's Settings → Privacy options.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.

2. Who is responsible for your information

Zenze Dev is the data controller (the party that decides how and why your information is processed) for the Games. Zenze Dev is based in Thailand. You can reach us at zenzedev@gmail.com. For some advertising purposes, Google acts as an independent controller (see section 5).

3. Information we collect

Information stored only on your device

Games save progress, completed levels, scores, streaks, hints, purchase entitlements and settings (such as language, sound and accessibility options) on your device. In Games without an online account, this information is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the Game, clear its data, or use "Reset progress" where available. Because those Games have no accounts, progress is not transferred to a new device; non-consumable purchases can be restored with "Restore purchases".

Information collected automatically

Depending on the Game and your choices, the services in section 5 may collect:

  • Device and app identifiers: advertising ID, app set ID, Firebase installation ID, app instance ID;
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, app version, approximate country or region (derived from the IP address);
  • Game activity: events such as levels started or completed, features used and session length;
  • Ad information: ads shown and interactions with them, and your consent choices;
  • Crash and error reports: stack traces, device state and app version. Crash reports never include words you type or select in a Game;
  • Purchase information: purchase records handled by the app store (for example order ID, product and purchase token). We never receive your payment card details or your store account password.

Information you give us

  • If you email us (for example for support, a refund question or a data request), we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.
  • If a Game lets you share something (for example a picture or a result) through your device's share menu, it is sent to the app or person you choose; we do not receive it.

Information for Games with online accounts is described in section 6.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the Games, save progress and deliver and restore purchases;
  • to show ads that fund free Games, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent ad fraud;
  • to understand how the Games are played so we can improve difficulty, content and features;
  • to find and fix crashes and errors, and to adjust Game settings remotely;
  • to keep Games, accounts and servers secure and fair, and to prevent cheating, abuse and fraud;
  • to answer your messages and requests;
  • to comply with the law and app store rules, and to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not use your information to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Games with online accounts

Some Games save your progress online. Statements elsewhere on this page that a Game has "no accounts" or keeps data "only on your device" do not apply to these Games. In these Games:

  • Account. You are signed in automatically as an anonymous guest using Google Firebase Authentication. You do not enter a name, email address or password. Firebase creates a guest user ID and the Game keeps a sign-in token on your device. Nothing is sent to our server, and no sign-in starts, until you have accepted the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy in the Game.
  • Server data. The Game talks to our backend, which runs on Cloudflare Workers with a Cloudflare D1 database. Each request carries your Firebase ID token, which the server verifies. The server stores:
    • an internal account ID (random; not your Firebase user ID) and the link between it and your Firebase user ID;
    • your game progress and results;
    • short-lived technical records: recent request records (so a repeated request is answered the same way) and a per-day count of your game calls;
    • purchase history: for each purchase, the purchase token, product ID, order ID, what was granted, its state (for example granted or refunded) and timestamps.
  • Purchase verification. The server sends the purchase token to the Google Play Developer API to confirm the purchase, grant the item and detect refunds.
  • Unless the Game's store listing says otherwise, Games with online accounts do not include AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or Remote Config.

To delete an online account and its data, see section 11.

7. Your consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Games that show ads display a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform, which follows the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. The Game checks your choices each time it starts. You can consent to personalised ads and related processing; decline, in which case you may still see non-personalised or limited ads; or choose individual purposes and vendors. Where a Game shows a privacy message in another region, the same choices apply.

Firebase Analytics data is collected only if you have given consent; if you decline or have not yet answered, analytics collection stays off. You can change or withdraw your choices at any time in the Game's Settings → Privacy options. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened.

On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads across apps, in Settings → Google → Ads (wording may vary by device). Uninstalling a Game stops all collection by that Game.

  • Advertising partners, namely Google AdMob and the ad technology providers it works with, according to your consent choices;

We do not sell your personal information for money. Showing personalised ads may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some US state laws; you can opt out as described in section 7 and section 14.

10. How long we keep information

  • On-device data: until you uninstall the Game, clear its data or reset progress.
  • Firebase Analytics: user-level data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • AdMob and Google Play: according to Google's own retention policies.
  • Online accounts: account and progress data is kept while your account is in use and deleted within 30 days after you ask us to delete it. Recent request records are removed automatically after 7 days, and old per-day call counts are removed automatically.
  • Purchase records: kept for as long as app store rules and accounting, tax or other legal requirements require, then deleted.
  • Emails to us: kept as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, normally no longer than 2 years.

11. Deleting your data

  • Games without accounts: uninstall the Game or clear its data to remove everything stored on your device. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data linked to your device, email us (see section 13).
  • Games with online accounts: email zenzedev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", the name of the Game, and any details that help us find your account (for example the order ID of a purchase from your Google Play receipt). Because accounts are anonymous guest accounts, please send the request from the device you play on. We will delete your account record, game progress and results, and the purchase records we hold about it, within 30 days, except where we must keep purchase records as described in section 10. Where a Game offers a delete-account option in its settings, you can also use that.

12. Security

We protect information with technical and organisational measures suited to the Games. Data sent between a Game and our servers or service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Requests to our servers are authenticated with signed tokens. Access to server data, service consoles and keys is limited to Zenze Dev, protected by access controls, and used only for the purposes in this policy. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly and notify you and the authorities where the law requires it if a breach affects you.
