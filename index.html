1. Summary

  • The Apps have no accounts or sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers.
  • The Apps show ads through Google AdMob, use Google Firebase to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes, and sell optional items through Google Play Billing.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for your choices before personalised ads are shown or analytics are collected, and you can change them at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

2. Information stored on your device

The Apps save game progress, completed levels, daily streaks, hints, purchase entitlements and settings (such as sound and accessibility options) on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the App, clear its data, or use "Reset progress" where available. Because there are no accounts, progress is not transferred to a new device; purchases can be restored with "Restore purchases".

4. Your consent choices (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are located in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the Apps display a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform, which follows the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. The App checks your choices each time it starts. You can:

  • Consent to personalised ads and related processing;
  • Decline, in which case you may still see non-personalised or limited ads; or
  • Choose individual purposes and vendors.

Firebase Analytics data is collected only if you have given consent; if you decline or have not yet answered, analytics collection stays off. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the App.

6. Your controls on Android

  • Change ad and analytics consent: Settings → Privacy options in the App (shown where required).
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads across apps: Android Settings → Google → Ads (wording may vary by device).
  • Uninstall the App to stop all collection by the App and the services above.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the Apps have no accounts and we hold no data that directly identifies you, we may be unable to link a request to you; please include your device's Firebase or advertising identifiers if you want us to look for specific data. You can also use Google's privacy tools. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Data retention

  • On-device data: until you uninstall the App, clear its data, or reset progress.
  • Firebase Analytics: user-level data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • AdMob and Google Play: according to Google's own retention policies.

9. Children

The Apps are not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age that applies in your country, such as 16 in some EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Apps, please contact us and we will help remove it.

10. International transfers

Google may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. Google relies on approved safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's store listing or update notes.
